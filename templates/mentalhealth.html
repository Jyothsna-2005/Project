🌿 Daily Wellness Tips

  • Stay hydrated
  • Take short study breaks
  • Practice deep breathing
  • Get enough sleep

🧘 Stress Management

  • Try 4-7-8 breathing
  • Write your thoughts
  • Listen to calming music

📚 Study-Life Balance

  • Create a realistic schedule
  • Take guilt-free breaks
  • Include hobbies

💬 Positive Thinking

  • Practice self-affirmations
  • Celebrate small wins
  • Avoid comparisons

🚨 When to Seek Help

  • Constant stress or sadness
  • Sleep problems
  • Loss of interest

Seeking help is a sign of strength 💙
